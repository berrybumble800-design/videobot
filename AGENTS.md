# Rules for making the video
- Make a short animated explainer video (30-60 seconds, 1280x720, 24 fps).
- Tools you may use: Python, Pillow, numpy, edge-tts (free voice), ffmpeg.
- Plan a short script first, then draw frames with Pillow, make the voice with edge-tts, join with ffmpeg.
- Save the final file EXACTLY as output/final.mp4 (create the output folder).
- Do not ask questions. Finish the whole job yourself.
